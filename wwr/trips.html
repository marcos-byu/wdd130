Trip Highlights

Los Dientes del Diablo — Half Day

A fast-paced half-day trip through a series of technical Class IV rapids. Great for rafters who already have some river experience and want a bigger adrenaline hit without committing a full day.

Big Mallard — Full Day

A full day on the river featuring wide open stretches and one of the river's biggest named rapids. Lunch is served riverside between runs, making this a favorite for families and first-time full-day rafters.

Zambezi Gorge Expedition — 3 Days

Our flagship multi-day expedition through the legendary Zambezi Gorge. Expect sustained Class V rapids, riverside camping, and experienced guides every step of the way. Recommended for experienced rafters only.
